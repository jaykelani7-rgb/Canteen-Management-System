# Canteen OS food photograph library

These are photographs of the listed foods, downloaded from verified Wikimedia Commons file pages. They are illustrative photographs, not photographs taken of this canteen's actual servings. No third-party hotlinks are required at runtime.

The optimised photographs retain their listed licences. Attribution, source and licence links must be exposed to app users through photo credits or image metadata. CC BY-SA covers the photographs and their adaptations; the photographs are separate from application code.

Modifications: proportional resizing to at most 960 pixels, EXIF orientation correction, metadata removal and WebP compression. No watermark removal, generated food or invented image associations.
